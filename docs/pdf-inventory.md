# PDF inventory — Avadhut Classes Brand Identity Guidelines (Edition 01)

Source: `Avadhut-Classes-Brand-Guidelines (1).pdf` — 29 pages, landscape 1200 × 750 pt.
Method: text extracted with MuPDF (fonts and sizes logged per line), every page rendered to PNG and
inspected visually, logos re-rendered from vector artwork to transparent PNG, photos extracted.

Recurring on pages 2–28 (running header/footer):
- Header left: small primary logo · "BRAND GUIDELINES". Header right: section label (e.g. "01 · BRAND STORY").
- Footer left: "CLASSES RIGHT TOH FUTURE BRIGHT". Footer right: page number (Avadhut Purple; Yellow on dark pages).
- Every page title is Poppins Medium ~33 pt in Deep Plum, followed by a short Foundation Yellow rule.
- Section labels are tracked (letter-spaced) uppercase Inter, in Purple / Deep Blue / grey.

Fonts seen in the file: Poppins Light / Regular / Medium (incl. Devanagari), Inter (embedded as Type3 —
identified visually), Lora Italic, Futura (inside the logo artwork). LiberationSans-Bold appears only
inside the "Don't crowd the clear space" misuse example ("ADMISSION OPEN!"); FreeSans only for the
"मराठी" label glyphs on p19. These two are artefacts of the misuse/label artwork, not brand fonts.

## Page 17 — 05 · Colour — "Using colour"
- PROPORTION bar: 55% White & Chalk · 25% Purple · 12% (Blue) · 8% (Yellow).
- "Keep layouts light and calm, with plenty of white space. **Purple** leads in headlines and key panels; **blue** carries
  information and calls to action; **yellow** is the spark, used for rules, rungs and small highlights."
- "Never set yellow text on white, and keep official blue for large type. For small blue text, use Deep Blue."

## Page 22 — 08 · Voice & Messaging — "Voice & tone"
- ONE VOICE, FOUR TONES:
  Students — Encouraging, energetic, simple — "Small steps every day. You've got this."
  Parents — Reassuring, respectful, transparent — "Your child's monthly test report is ready. Let's review it together."
  Notices — Clear, formal, complete — "Std 8 Science test on Saturday, 10 a.m. Syllabus: Chapters 4–6."
  Celebrations — Proud, humble, grateful — "Congratulations to our Std 10 students. Your discipline paid off."
- ALWAYS: Speak like a good teacher: calm, organised, kind · Lead with the student, not with us · Use real facts: standards,
  subjects, timings · Keep sentences short; one idea at a time · Mix English and Marathi naturally where it helps.
- NEVER: "Guaranteed 100% marks", rank or result promises · Too many exclamation marks or ALL CAPS · Fear-based lines:
  "Don't let your child fall behind!" · Comparing or criticising other classes · Slang that parents may find careless.
